Why this keeps happening

Paraphrased from studio owners reviewing their own software on G2 and Capterra, 2025 to 2026.

It's buried
The report exists somewhere in the software. Finding it means digging through a dozen menus you don't visit otherwise.
It's not built for you
The numbers are there, but reading them like a spreadsheet isn't how most owners think about their studio.
Nobody acts on it
A list of names doesn't call anyone. Someone has to turn it into a text, a class change, a next step.
