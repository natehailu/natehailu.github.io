About

Two years of watching systems fail up close.

I work airline operations at Lubbock, handling passengers whose plans have just come apart. Before that I spent two years at a Level 1 trauma center moving patients through a 500-bed hospital and keeping their records straight in Epic.

Both jobs are the same job underneath. Limited capacity, a queue, a prioritization rule, and a recovery process that buckles when volume spikes. I got interested in measuring that rather than just absorbing it.

I have a psychology degree with a business minor from Texas Tech, which turns out to be a useful pair of lenses for operational work. One asks why people behave the way they do under stress. The other asks what it costs.
